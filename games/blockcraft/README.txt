BLOCKCRAFT - offline version
1. Unzip this folder anywhere.
2. Double-click Blockcraft.html (Chrome, Edge or Firefox on a computer).
3. Click Singleplayer > Create New World.
Sounds: all 2,780 sounds are built in (the "sounds" folder). Keep it next to Blockcraft.html. They load automatically.
Controls: WASD walk, Space jump, Shift sneak, Ctrl sprint, E inventory, T chat, F3 debug, F2 screenshot, Esc pause.
View bobbing is off by default (Options > Video Settings to turn it on).
New: Nether (build a 4x5 obsidian frame, light it with flint and steel, stand inside),
redstone (dust, levers, buttons, pressure plates, torches, lamps, TNT, pistons, note blocks),
mobs: zombie, skeleton, creeper, spider, enderman, slime, wolf (tame with bones), ghast, zombified piglin.
Controller button icons: Kenney Input Prompts (CC0, kenney.nl).
On phones and tablets the game runs in Bedrock mode with touch controls.
Font: "Slightly Improved Font" by latvian.dev is used for all text.
Mods: title screen > Mods. No mods are installed. Import your own (.js, or .zip with mod.js / modpack.json).
Third person: F5 (touch: F5 button, controller: D-pad up).
Keep the "sounds" and "packs" folders next to Blockcraft.html. Sounds and packs load in the background after the title screen appears.

Texture packs: Bare Bones (default) and Vanilla (original Minecraft textures). Switch in Options > Resource Packs.
